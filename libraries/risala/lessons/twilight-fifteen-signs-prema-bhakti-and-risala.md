## Why Twilight Welcomes More Than One Tongue

At midday, a workshop speaks the language of gauges, calipers, and column limits.
At **twilight** -- Sanskrit *sandhya* (the sacred seam between light and night),
Arabic *shafaq* and *maghrib* (the rose-amber afterglow when tools are wiped
clean), Tamil *malai-ppozhudu*, Andalusian *sobremesa* -- the heart turns from
*how fast did we build?* to *whom did we love through this work?*

Following [`01-brahman-paramatman-bhagavan-and-prema-bhakti-in-three-arabics.md`](01-brahman-paramatman-bhagavan-and-prema-bhakti-in-three-arabics.md)
and [`02-the-twelve-zodiac-signs-and-the-skys-craft-in-three-arabics.md`](02-the-twelve-zodiac-signs-and-the-skys-craft-in-three-arabics.md),
this table never collapses distinct traditions into a flat equation. Instead, it
sets **Bhakti-rasa** (*Shastra*: Andal, Rupa Goswami, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu) and
**Mahabbah / Hikmah** (*Risala*: Quranic *buruj*, al-Ghazali, Ibn 'Arabi) beside
each other as two lamps lighting the same fifteen-step porch.
