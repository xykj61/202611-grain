## Plain-English Welcome (Zero Prior Sanskrit or Coding Required!)

Every unfamiliar word in this multi-year program is introduced in everyday English the moment you meet it:

- ***Shastra* (Sacred Science / Systematic Teaching):** Clear, testable texts that ask *how do we know what is true?* and *how do we live with joy and care?*
- ***Bhakti* & *Prema* (Devotion & Ripened Love):** *Bhakti* is active, caring service toward the Beloved and all living beings; *prema* is that care ripened into spontaneous, unbreakable love.
- ***Rasa* (Living Aesthetic Taste / Emotional Flavor):** Just as music or painting carries a felt mood -- wonder, friendship, tenderness, courage -- **Rupa Goswami** mapped how human hearts experience relationship at its highest beauty.
- ***Sandarbha* (Woven Garland of Proofs):** Six systematic books by **Jiva Goswami** organizing the entire philosophy of *Srimad-Bhagavatam* and the Vedas from first principles (*how we know* -> *what is real* -> *how we act* -> *the joyful goal*).

### Year 2 -- The Ocean of Relational Beauty: Rupa Goswami's *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu* + Creative Systems
- **Heart Reading:** **Rupa Goswami's** *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu* (*The Nectar-Ocean of Devotional Aesthetics*), divided into its four directional waves:
  1. *Purva-vibhaga* (Eastern Wave -- foundational *sadhana* / daily craft discipline, mirroring **TAME Core** & **Calfive** rhythm!),
  2. *Dakshina-vibhaga* (Southern Wave -- the ingredients of *rasa*: *vibhava* context, *anubhava* expression, *sattvika* authenticity, *vyabhichari* passing hues, *sthayibhava* enduring heart-tone),
  3. *Paschima-vibhaga* (Western Wave -- the five primary relationships: peace *shanta*, service *dasya*, friendship *sakhya*, parental care *vatsalya*, and radiant belovedness *madhurya*),
  4. *Uttara-vibhaga* (Northern Wave -- the seven secondary harmonies and how moods harmonize vs. clash).
- **Craft Twin in Grain:** **Brushstroke/Skate UI**, **Resonance Composition**, **Linengrow** vegan patronage, and **Dimeroll** double-entry stewardship.
- **What You Build:** A full zero-spyware **Linengrow** zine & music studio with **Mudra** privacy-preserving patronage receipts!

### Year 3 -- Epistemology & Ontology: Jiva Goswami's *Sat-Sandarbhas* I-IV + The Glow Mill
- **Heart Reading:** The first four *Sandarbhas* of **Jiva Goswami** (referencing [`../../study/shastra/sandarbhas/`](../../study/shastra/README.md)):

  4. ***Krishna-sandarbha*** (*Personhood as the Fountainhead of Law & Grace* -- *achintya-bhedabheda*, simultaneous oneness and difference, like Frenkel's one cup casting two shadows!).
