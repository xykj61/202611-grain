## 2. Designing Streets and Bridges That Drink the Rain

Traditional human-scale urbanism (honoring Christopher Alexander and Lewis
Mumford) treats every street, plaza, and bridge approach as an active partner in
the local watershed ([`../../civic-ecology/`](../../civic-ecology/README.md)):

1. **Permeable Pavements and Tree-Trench Bioswales:** Sidewalks, courtyard
   lanes, and parking shoulders use porous stone pavers over gravel recharge
   beds planted with deep-rooted shade trees, letting storm surges replenish
   groundwater right where rain falls.
2. **Regional Mass Timber and Plant-Fiber Insulation (`WOOD`):** Cross-laminated
   regional timber and biocyclic fiber insulation store carbon inside schools,
   civic halls, and homes while delivering warm `>= R-20` envelopes and measured
   `>= 2.0x` structural safety factors.
3. **Generational Performance Bonds in `Colloquium`:** Builders escrow multi-year
   performance bonds alongside independent structural and hydrological sign-off
   (`324/405` constitutional lock), aligning construction quality at ribbon-
   cutting with decades of peaceful community service.
