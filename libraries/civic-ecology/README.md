## Welcome to Civic Ecology

`libraries/civic-ecology/` teaches and models how American towns, counties,
highways, and farms can cool summer ground, recharge aquifers, and welcome steady
rainfall deep into the heartland by aligning public incentives
([`../../context/CIVIC_STYLE.md`](../../context/CIVIC_STYLE.md)) with the living
physics of water, soil, and multi-layer forest canopies.
