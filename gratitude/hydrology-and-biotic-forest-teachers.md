## Teachers and Researchers We Thank

In [`../libraries/civic-ecology/`](../libraries/civic-ecology/README.md), Grain
expresses watershed restoration, ground-cooling canopy design, and continental
moisture transport purely in our own first-principles `Teacher + Radiant + Civic`
vocabulary via [`../context/SILO_TECHNIQUE.md`](../context/SILO_TECHNIQUE.md).
Here in `gratitude/`, we warmly thank the scientists, hydrologists, and
ecological educators whose public teaching illuminated these physical laws:

- **Anastassia Makarieva and Victor Gorshkov** -- for articulating the physics
  of atmospheric water-vapor condensation above multi-layer forest canopies and
  showing how intact forests draw ocean moisture deep into continental interiors.
- **Watershed Restoration Educators and Regenerative Hydrologists** (including
  public educational films on reversing watershed runoff cycles, Rajendra Singh's
  community rainwater harvesting, Sepp Holzer's retention landscapes, and Andrew
  Millison's visual hydrology teaching) -- for making soil infiltration, spring
  revival, and sponge landscapes legible to everyday citizens.
- **Helen Atthowe and Frances Gonzales** -- for demonstrating biocyclic,
  plant-nourished living soil that holds water, cools summer ground, and feeds
  communities generously.
