## Asana

Thank you, Asana, for demonstrating that project management can
be beautiful. Your board view, your timeline, your attention to
the visual rhythm of tasks moving through columns -- these showed
the world that work tracking is not a spreadsheet chore but a
living practice. Pulp's Living Board carries this conviction
forward: a board where every card is both a unit of work and a
visual canvas, dressed wholly in Grain's own vocabulary.

## Instagram

Thank you, Instagram, for proving that a chronological visual
feed can hold an entire community's attention with warmth and
immediacy. Your early insight -- that photographs arranged in
time tell a story no text summary can match -- seeded the idea
that project feeds should feel the same way. Pulp's Social Feed
is a chronological, append-only, Blaze-signed river of visual
entries where every update carries a picture and an honest
timestamp, inspired by the conviction that visual storytelling
belongs inside the workspace, not beside it.
