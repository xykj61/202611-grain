2. **Hardware-gated `Earpiece`:** Voice capture (`earpiece_core.rye`) refuses to

   bystander-consent lamp is lit, and wipes ring buffers via `secureZero`.

   requests zero `INTERNET` ad hooks and zero ambient `RECORD_AUDIO`.
