## 1. Sameness Is the Macro

Compress the codebase by making parts publicly similar rather than inventing
hidden macros, reflection, or private dialects:

- **One value model:** `Brix` recipes, `Kyri` ledgers, and wire envelopes share
  explicit strings, integers, booleans, lists, and records.
- **One pure fold:** Application, studio, and ledger state evolve as pure folds
  over content-addressed `Blaze` facts (`sum(nets) == 0` in `Dimeroll`).
- **One UI & Studio brain (`modules/comlink/vantage/`):** `Carry` (`Carry
  Tablet`, `Carry Mobile`) and `Station` desktop surfaces are implemented
  strictly inside `Vantage`. Platform hosts (`terrarium/app/` Swift,
  `terrarium/carry-tablet/` Kotlin JNI, and WASM) are dumb-glass I/O pipes.
  `Resonance` unlocks its full 3-Zone Desktop DAW in `Vantage Station` posture
  and presents a Field Stem Sketchpad in `Carry` over the same `.brix` session.

## 2. Custody First & Aparigraha

- Keys stay in the citizen's hands (`Kumara` / `VaultedGeminiKey` echo refusal).
- Devices forget secrets when power drops (`Blazepod` volatile `tally-ram` &
  `Earpiece` `secureZero`).
- Business models serve human flourishing without ad rent or surveillance
  (*Aparigraha* & `Seva`).
- Universal CLI & cross-distro package name: **`grain-ww`** (`grain_ww` /
  `com.grainww` where language syntax forbids hyphens).
