## Anuradha Nakshatra and the Spirit of Mitra

In the ancient **Taittiriya Brahmana nakshatra sukta** (enumerated from Krittika in [`../classical-vedic-astrology/20260810_nakshatra-outfits-and-the-sundial-face.md`](../classical-vedic-astrology/20260810_nakshatra-outfits-and-the-sundial-face.md), Seat **15**; Seat **17** in Ashvini-first almanacs, spanning `3 deg 20'` to `16 deg 40'` sidereal Scorpio), **Anuradha** (*"following Radha"* -- the star of devotion following the spark of Vishakha) is presided over by **Mitra**, the Vedic Aditya of friendship, sacred covenant, and gentle, reliable harmony.

Anuradha's traditional emblem is a **lotus blooming upon deep water**: the flower rises clean and fragrant while thanking the submerged stem and root mud that nourished it.

In Grain's engineering discipline, **Mitra** gives its name to our middle depth of removal:

1. **Accrete-Never-Break (Molt):** When a cleaner design, module home, or Glow core supersedes an elder file, we grow the new mutant **beside** the elder first so zero callers break mid-flight.
2. **Prepare in [`shed-prep.md`](shed-prep.md):** Once the living mutant stands proven on metal, we register the elder here as a **prepared fossil** -- naming its living replacement, its remaining inbound links, and the exact witness that proves every promise has already transferred hands.
3. **Door-Approved Shed:** Only when Keaton speaks the explicit word at the door do we lay a signed walk-back nib in [`../construction/CHECKPOINTS.md`](../construction/CHECKPOINTS.md) and release the fossil cleanly.

A knife severs; a **Mitra shed** keeps every covenant first so letting go feels like gratitude.
