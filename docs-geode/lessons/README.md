## Why These Eight Lessons Walk Together

Before touching advanced compiler passes or network cryptography, a newcomer deserves a clear mental map of **how an entire computer fits together** from power-on to a pixel on glass:

1. **[`foundations-of-the-house.md`](foundations-of-the-house.md)** -- Walks all eight steps in one calm sitting:
   - *Lessons 1-2 (The House From Above & The Front Door):* How `languages/`, `modules/`, `blazepod/`, `docs-geode/`, and `yonder/` divide every concern cleanly.
   - *Lessons 3-5 (The Why, Joy Has a Rank, & Chronological Order):* Why bounded systems feel joyful, and how `YYYYMMDD.HHMMSS` chronological stamps keep time honest without semantic-versioning arguments.
   - *Lesson 6 (Two Pockets -- `modules/tally/`):* How static memory gardens replace unpredictable heap allocation.
   - *Lesson 7 (Dawn and Kernels -- `modules/aurora/` & `modules/caravan/`):* How a RISC-V hart wakes up and supervises isolated citizen divisions (`modules/pond/`).
   - *Lesson 8 (The Painted Face -- `modules/brushstroke/`):* How pure state folds become crisp cells, QOI sprites, and 60 fps surfaces.
