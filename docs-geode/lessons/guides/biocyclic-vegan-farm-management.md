# Guide -- Biocyclic Vegan Farm Management

**Stage:** Bloom Practice Layer
**Voice:** Teacher & Civic

---

## 1. Soil First, Zero Exploitation

A Grain-stewarded farm grows nourishing food without animal exploitation,
synthetic pesticides, or extractive debt. Using **biocyclic vegan humus soil**
principles, plant-based compost matures into stable, moisture-retentive humus
beds that feed vegetables, orchards, grains, and legumes year after year.

## 2. Four Daily Ledger Verbs in Dimeroll

Every field event enters your local `Dimeroll` book on your **Carry Tablet**
(readable in bright noon sun on a 60 Hz reflective RLCD):

1. `compost` -- weigh green/brown plant windrows and log temperature turns.
2. `plant`   -- record bed ID, seed lot, row feet, and expected harvest window.
3. `harvest` -- weigh clean yield per bed in integer grams or kilograms.
4. `sell`    -- balance customer cooperative boxes with double-entry `MALA`.

Because every `Dimeroll` entry satisfies `sum(nets) == 0`, inventory and cash
flow reconcile at sundown with zero spreadsheet drift.
