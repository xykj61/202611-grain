# Guide -- Cooperative Distribution and Routes

**Stage:** Bloom Practice Layer
**Voice:** Teacher & Civic

---

## 1. Direct Routes Without Middlemen Rent

Conventional food chains lose half a harvest's value to opaque brokers and ad
platforms. In Grain's cooperative model, neighborhood pickup rooms (`Mitra`
hubs) aggregate weekly orders into calm, predictable point-to-point milk-runs
(for oat milk, fresh greens, milled flour, and seasonal fruit!).

## 2. Offline-Resilient Route Manifests

1. Neighborhood households place weekly basket requests over `Comlink`.
2. Before dawn, the route steward syncs the day's manifest onto a **Daylight
   DC-1 Carry Tablet** via `VantageApp` (`Dimeroll` Tab).
3. Even through rural dead-zones with zero cellular reception, every delivery
   stop signs off locally into `Blaze` and settles automatically when the van
   returns to the farm's **Blazepod**.
