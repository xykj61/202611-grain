# Guide -- Steward Coordination and Rail Cadence

**Stage:** Bloom Practice Layer
**Voice:** Teacher & Civic

---

## 1. Pointing-and-Calling Clarity

Great rail systems achieve calm safety not through surveillance, but through
clear timetables, physical checklists, and **pointing-and-calling** (*shisa
kanko*) at every handoff. Grain teams coordinate the same way:

- **Explicit Handoffs:** Every shift ends with a 5-line `Kyri` baton naming
  what is green, what is warming, and the single next step.
- **Humane Rhythm:** Four focused morning hours in the field or workshop,
  shared plant-based lunch, unhurried afternoon study (*Bloom & Shastra*), and
  quiet rest after dusk.
- **Zero Bystander Surveillance:** Under our **Civic Glasswall** rule, crews
  coordinate by explicit push-to-talk `Earpiece` batons -- never GPS tracking
  collars or always-on microphones.
