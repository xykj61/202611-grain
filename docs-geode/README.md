## Why `docs-geode/` Instead of Plain `docs/`

A conventional `docs/` folder quietly promises a finished, shrink-wrapped product. For an early adopter arriving while Grain is actively forging its compiler, bare-metal RISC-V enclaves, and citizen modules, that silent promise would be less than fully honest.

A **geode** tells the whole truth in one word:

- **Rough volcanic stone outside:** parts of this tree are still molten, growing, and re-cutting their seams in public daylight.
- **Pure crystal inside:** every command, version stamp, and architectural claim admitted into `docs-geode/` has been run on real metal first.

By retiring plain `docs/` into [`../yonder/docs-old/`](../yonder/README.md) (sweeping away legacy 4-letter waymark pages like `STOA` and `TUBE`) and preserving the first geode draft in [`../yonder/docs-geode-old/`](../yonder/README.md), this single `docs-geode/` room gives newcomers and enterprise evaluators one trustworthy doorway.
