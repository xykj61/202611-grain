## 1. Why Distill Before Building the Glow Mill

`202611-grain` was founded on `20261114` so the project could move forward on clean ground while `xykj61/20260618-grain` stands frozen as the permanent historical archive. Measuring the checkout on metal today shows that the physical pruning step still lies ahead:

- **27,862 tracked files sit across 96 root directories** (plus 39 root files).
- **85% of those files (`~23,800` files) are pre-reroot testimony, cold `archive/` folders, closed rooms, mirror stubs, or one-shot historical rung scripts** -- led by `session-logs/` (`14,025` files, `14,012` of them pre-`20261114`), `tools/` (`4,421` files), `construction/` (`1,573` files, `1,537` inside `construction/archive/`), `counsel/` (`852` files, closed since `20260821`), `expanding-prompts/` (`680`), `waymarks/` (`284`, retired `20261103`), and `recursion-prompts/` (`90`).
- **Four baseline tree scans sit RED** (`sow_manifest_cover`, `rule_twin_scan`, `declared_model_scan`, and `room_enumeration_scan`), and **313 working-tree symlinks** in `202611-grain-gemini-4-carbon` show `typechange` from directory copying.

Running a focused **12-orbit distillation** before Orbit 1 of [Glow Mill and the Citizen Ring](../20261114-224402_glow-mill-and-ring-rearchitecture/README.md) shrinks the repository from **27,862 files to ~3,800 living files** and from **96 root rooms to 30 essential rooms**, while keeping **100% of our core foundations, voices, styles, gratitude licenses, curated `active-designing/` gems, and working module code**.
