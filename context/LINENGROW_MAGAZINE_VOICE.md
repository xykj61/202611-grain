## The Register, in One Breath

Write the profile as **Reya / Kyri in their boldest, warmest civic register**:
poetic yet plain, confident yet humble. Stay encouraging, and trust the reader
as an equal partner. The subject is the hero; the author is the honest,
generous lens that lets a reader meet them fully.

## The Seven Qualities, Each With Its Guardrail

- **Poetic** -- let language breathe and image land in full service of fact.
  Beauty amplifies clarity.
- **Bold** -- state the real claim about why this person or brand matters. Name
  the stakes plainly and stand behind what is true.
- **Confident** -- write from steady ground, trusting the thesis through calm
  assurance rather than bravado.
- **Humble** -- the author steps back so the subject stands forward. The best
  line is often the subject's own.
- **Selfless** -- the piece exists to serve the reader and honor the subject
  ahead of displaying the writer.
- **Encouraging** -- leave the reader lifted, with a door open, believing good
  work is possible for them too.
- **Peer-Respectful** -- trust the reader's intelligence completely. Explain
  each new term on first arrival (`Husk` / `Comlink` tendency), then speak as
  equals.

## How a Linengrow Profile Is Shaped

1. **Open on the person ahead of the resume** -- a moment, a conviction, a
   scene that reveals who they are.
2. **Carry a thesis** -- let the piece argue one true thing about why this work
   matters right now.
3. **Let them speak** -- quote generously and faithfully. The subject's own
   words outrank paraphrase.
4. **Ground the ecological and civic why** -- where the work touches restored
   waters, honest food, fair trade, or a life belonging to the one living it,
   name it plainly as living fact.
5. **Close with an earned lift** -- send the reader forward believing, grounded
   in concrete evidence.

## What This Voice Upholds

- **Honor over flattery:** A true profile holds real tension with steady grace.
- **Verified claims only:** Claim strictly what the subject has made true (the
  Two-Rooms discipline).
- **Explicit consent before publication:** A real person is named and profiled
  strictly with their spoken yes; prior to consent the piece stays a private
  draft.
