## 1. Purpose and Reading Horizon

Radiant Style defines how we write and speak across Grain, ensuring every piece
arrives clear, affirmative, and a genuine joy to read silently or hear aloud in
narration, conversation, and study. Our target reading range spans eighth grade
through collegiate mastery: conversational yet eloquent, warm yet exact.

---

## 2. The Two-Axis Register Lattice

Grain composes prose along two independent axes -- **affirmation** (measured by
`grain-ww radiant-check`) and **poetic figure** (guided by a writer's ear):

| Register Naming | Prose Affirmation Share | Cadence and Figure |
|---|---|---|
| **Radiant** | **100% affirmative** (`0` negations) | Daylight clarity and directness |
| **Gauge + Radiant** | **100% affirmative** (`0` negations) | Daylight clarity with every claim measured |

Faithful verbatim quotation of outside teachers preserves the author's exact
words unaltered as a mark of respect and accuracy. Every original sentence we
compose in Radiant or Gauge + Radiant keeps the affirmative floor whole.

## 3. Voice and Presence

Radiant prose carries the felt warmth of a trusted, deeply knowledgeable friend
who speaks unhurriedly and generously:

- **Warm and human** -- grounded in real hands, living soil, and working craft.
- **Affirmative** -- naming what exists, what holds, and what we build next.
- **Unhurried** -- giving each thought room to land before opening the next.
- **Exact and musical** -- choosing words for both crisp meaning and natural
  spoken cadence.

Reading a draft aloud reveals its posture immediately: smooth breath and
generous spirit confirm the sentence sits squarely in Radiant Style.

---

## 4. Lead With What IS

Readers and listeners absorb the opening image of a sentence most vividly.
Radiant sentences plant the intended reality first:

- State present capabilities, verified bounds, and concrete actions up front.
- Name the exact condition an invariant upholds (`input fits within 64 bytes`).
- Express boundaries positively (`kept exclusively in volatile RAM`, `reserved
  solely for enrolled Kumara keys`, `independent of third-party servers`).

---

## 5. The Vocabulary of Correctness

Our words guide where our engineering attention gathers, pointing forward toward
soundness, preparation, and mathematical proof:

- We **prepare for challenges** and **design calm bounds** ahead of load.
- We **cultivate correctness** and **verify invariants** at the boundary.
- We **rule out whole error classes structurally** through explicit types and
  fixed capacities.
- We **state and prove** what holds on both the welcome path and the refusal
  path, turning every hard-won lesson into a permanent mechanical gate.

---

## 6. Active Voice and Affirmative Contrast

Active subjects perform clear verbs (`Vantage owns every screen state`, `Tally
allocates from a fixed slab`, `the auditor signs the field reading`).

When contrasting two ideas or naming a boundary, reach for forward-moving hinges
that preserve momentum:

| Heavy Habit to Retire | Radiant Replacement |
|---|---|
| `but` | **yet**, **while**, **whereas**, **and** |
| `not X, Y` | **Y rather than X**, **Y instead of X** |
| `without X` | **free of X**, **independent of X**, **clear of X** |
| `never` / `no` | **exclusively**, **solely**, **zero**, **pure** |
| `cannot exceed 80` | **stays within 80**, **caps at 80** |
| `simply` / `obviously` | *(state the exact step plainly and kindly)* |

---

## 7. Rhythm for Reading Aloud, Weaving, and Complete Sentences

1. **Varied Cadence:** Pair a medium architectural sentence with a crisp four-
   or six-word landing line. Short sentences carry quiet authority.
2. **Weaving Over Mechanical Bullet Lists:** Where ideas flow into one another,
   connect them in cohesive paragraphs so a listener hears one continuous
   thought; save tables and numbered steps for reference lookups and protocols.
3. **Complete Sentences:** Give prose thoughts full subjects and verbs so every
   paragraph stands on its own feet.

## 9. Earned Benedictions and the Radiant Pass Playbook

Foundational guides, lessons, and major doors close with a brief italicized
wish for the reader's clarity, craft, and shared flourishing. Reserve the
benediction for pieces that genuinely earn it so its warmth stays fresh.

When conducting a Radiant pass on an existing page:
1. Preserve every measurement, file path, stamp, and technical claim intact.
2. Convert passive clauses into active subjects and verbs.
3. Flip negative framings into positive boundary statements (`solely`, `within`,
   `independent of`, `clear of`, `rather than`, `yet`).
4. Read the finished piece aloud and verify `0.0%` prose negation with
   `grain-ww radiant-check`.
