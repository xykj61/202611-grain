## 1. The Three Words

**Simple, Lovable, Complete.** Three plain words guide how Grain releases work
into the world: small in scope, joyful to use, and whole inside its stated
bounds at every stage from the first metal witness to the full machine.

- **Lead with Simple:** Choose one clear job and bound its scope honestly. A
  small tool finishes cleanly and keeps every promise it makes. Simplicity
  opens the door to both lovability and completeness.
- **Let It Be Lovable:** Make every piece feel good in the hand on your own
  machine. Care shows in every detail; genuine affection grows from craft
  meeting completeness ahead of long feature checklists.
- **Make It Complete:** Close the loop end to end within the chosen scope. A
  small program that finishes its exact promise earns durable trust on day one.

## 3. Three Questions Ahead of Every Release

1. **Simple:** Does this hold one clear, honestly bounded scope?
2. **Lovable:** Would a person genuinely delight in running this today?
3. **Complete:** Does the loop close cleanly end to end within that scope?
