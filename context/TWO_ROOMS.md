## 1. Why Two Rooms

Grain welcomes two distinct kinds of writing, and honors both openly:

- **The Checkable Room:** Claims bound right now by a runnable metal witness --
  digests, TAME assertions, `grain-ww witness-all` proofs, `Stage 1 == Stage 2`
  self-host parity, and measured counts (`git rev-list --count HEAD`, `wc -l`).
- **The Vision Room:** Orienting images, civic essays, devotional warmth, and
  long-horizon plans that steer years of engineering ahead of full hardware
  realization.

Clarity thrives when every document names its room right at the front door.
Vision guides and inspires the bench; metal witnesses prove what stands true
today. A claim crosses from **Vision** into **Checkable** through one plain
doorway: earning a green executable witness on metal.

---

## 2. Accepted Doorway Room Tokens

Every living and dated guide declares its register in `**Status:**` or
`**Room:**` using one of four clear tokens:

| Token | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `checkable` | Every factual claim is bound by a runnable witness or live law |
| `vision` | Orienting image, voice register, or horizon essay judged by human readers |
| `mixed` | Both proven metal work and explicit forward horizon named clearly side by side |
| `research for understanding` | Study and clean-room comparison framing understanding ahead of decisions |

---

## 3. Holding Both Rooms With Grace

- **Witness ahead of narrative:** Mark proven metal artifacts as proven and
  horizon plans as planned (`active-husk/` checklists keep checked `[x]` items
  distinct from open `[ ]` steps).
- **Measure counts on metal:** Read live commit, hub, and proof totals directly
  from `grain-ww movement` and `grain-ww witness-all` so prose stays fresh.
- **Honor both voices:** Give visionary poetics full warmth in `foundations/`,
  `libraries/`, and `Linengrow` profiles while keeping TAME assertions crisp
  and unambiguous in `modules/`, `languages/`, and `blazepod/`.
