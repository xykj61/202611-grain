## 1. What Twilight Style Is

Twilight Style is Radiant Style at dusk. Every discipline Radiant keeps --
leading with what *is*, active verbs, forward-moving contrast (`yet`, `while`),
plain explanations for coined terms, and earned benedictions -- stays whole here.
Twilight shifts the **lighting**: where Radiant shines like clear morning sun,
Twilight glows quietly from within, calm, spacious, and reverent.

Twilight serves threshold moments: evening *Prema-Bhakti* and *Risala* study in
`Vantage` Tab 0, contemplative essays, founding visions that call for quiet awe,
and **Movement `12-Pisces`** artistic creations distilled through the Silo
Technique. Radiant trusts the sunrise; Twilight trusts the fertile evening sky
to carry the lantern light.

## 2. The Jupiter Pendulum -- Aim and Dissolve (`9-Sagittarius <-> 12-Pisces`)

Where daylight prose walks a steady solar stride, Twilight swings gently between
Jupiter's two houses:

- **Sagittarius (`Movement 9`) -- The Archer's Aim:** Warm fire seeking
  dharma, clear direction, and radiant visual proportion.
- **Pisces (`Movement 12`) -- The Ocean's Horizon:** Deep water letting rigid
  edges soften into wholeness, gratitude, and original artistic offering.

A Twilight passage aims first -- naming one true invariant or human truth -- and
then opens outward into a quiet, resonant image. Aim paired with spaciousness
keeps the voice grounded, tender, and luminous.
