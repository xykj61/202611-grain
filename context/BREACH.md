**The law:** accrete by default; breach solely by clear declaration. Spoken
short form: *accrete, sometimes breach.*

A **breach** declares itself up front, moves its entire declared scope in one
clean motion, preserves every byte and dated elder (archiving cleanly to
`yonder/` behind a walk-back nib in `construction/CHECKPOINTS.md`), and leaves
the far side simpler and clearer.

## The Six Promises

1. **Declared first:** Scope, reason, and destination shape stated plainly
   ahead of moving the first file.
2. **Every byte preserved:** Relocate and re-point cleanly; keep sealed
   testimony intact at its new or archived home.
3. **Finished inside scope:** Every live reference, symlink, CLI subcommand,
   and `.brix` entry updates in the same verified motion.
4. **Witnessed both sides:** An executable witness guards the rename and proves
   zero broken paths (`grain-ww witness-all` + `grain-ww audit-clean`).
5. **Measurably clearer:** Measure before, state the target, measure after,
   and record all three honestly.
6. **Reversible behind a walk-back nib:** Record the exact prior commit nib in
   [`../construction/CHECKPOINTS.md`](../construction/CHECKPOINTS.md) so every
   breach stays calmly reversible.

## The Breach Window

While consumers remain internal to the bench, mechanical renames stay crisp
and exact (`pulp -> husk` behind nib `42a9cff1`). Once outside dependents rely
on a public path, stewardship shifts smoothly to **Accrete-Keep-Whole** and
gentle migration bridges.
