## Speaking Across Public Difference

When Grain models budgets, ecological accounting, open hardware standards, or civic deliberation in [`../colloquium/`](../colloquium/README.md), **Civic Style** holds three rules:

1. **Steel-Man Every Serious Viewpoint:** State competing civic, economic, or regulatory positions in words their most thoughtful proponents would recognize as fair.
2. **Separate Mechanism From Value Choice:** Show the deterministic math (`Dimeroll` balanced transfers, bounded resource caps, ecological return ratios) separately from the human policy parameter a community chooses to set via `.brix`.
3. **Plain Public Accountability:** Use ordinary civic English free of partisan slogans so neighbors, cooperatives, municipalities, and enterprises across the United States and the world can audit every assumption in daylight.
