## 1. The One Sentence

**Redacting is siloing pointed outward.** To silo is to receive an idea from
the wider world and rebuild it in Grain's own voice so the universal principle
belongs cleanly to us. To redact is to take our *own* private particular -- a
real parcel, rate, or household figure -- and distill it into a general method
**anyone** can apply, letting the teaching travel freely while private
specifics stay safely at home.

---

## 2. What Redaction Releases & What It Preserves

- **Stays Safely at Home:** Personal names, entities, street addresses, exact
  parcel identifiers, account numbers, exact household balances, counterparties,
  and any single-search identifying clue.
- **Travels Publicly as Teaching:** The structural shape of the reasoning,
  every variable and direction of movement, decision thresholds, public base
  rates cited to open sources, and explicit boundary conditions showing where
  the method applies cleanly.
- **Level Over Exact Figure:** Transform an exact balance into a scale class,
  an exact rate into a live benchmark a reader looks up on the day, and a named
  year into a clear planning horizon.

---

## 3. Three Clarities of True Redaction

1. **Deeper Than Name-Swapping:** Rewriting at the level of general method
   ensures the page could serve a hundred different households equally well.
2. **More Exact in Reasoning:** With private anecdotes removed, the underlying
   logic, units, assumptions, and falsifiers carry the entire weight cleanly.
3. **Single-Stranded Source:** The private record stays inside `xy`, and the
   public redacted teaching projects cleanly from it rather than drifting as a
   separate hand-edited fork.
