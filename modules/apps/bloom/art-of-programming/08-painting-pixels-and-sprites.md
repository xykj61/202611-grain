## 1. Every Pixel Is Just a Friendly Number Grid

Screens look magical until you meet **`modules/brushstroke/`** (`brushstroke/image/` and `brushstroke/skate/`): a picture is simply a bounded rectangle of tiny colored squares (**pixels**) using our vegan **Linengrow** palette:

- **Oat** (`#F5F3EE`) -- warm plant-based light background,
- **Material Purple** (`#4A148C` / `#311B92`) -- deep royal ink,
- **Material Red** (`#D32F2F` / `#FF5252`) -- bright living accent.
