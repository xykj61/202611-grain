## 1. Putting Every Piece Together

Congratulations -- you have walked from opening `Terminal.app` for the very first time all the way to orchestrating AI collaborators safely!

In **`modules/harness/`** and **`modules/blaze/virtual_weave.rye`**:

1. **Virtual RAM Branches:** Up to 16 parallel **Gemini** and **Flare** helper agents can try out ideas on separate in-memory **Blaze** branches without touching a single file on your Mac's disk!
2. **Capability Masks & 3-Tier Review:** Each helper only holds the exact permissions you grant (`cap_skate_surface`, `cap_pure_compute`), and `harness_core` classifies every proposed change into *Tier 0 (comment-only)*, *Tier 1 (bounded code)*, or *Tier 2 (needs your human approval)*.
