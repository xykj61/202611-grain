## 1. Sound Waves Are Numbers Dancing 48,000 Times a Second

In **`modules/resonance/`**, your Mac's speakers play music by reading `48,000` numbers per second (**48 kHz PCM**):

- **`modules/resonance/composition/`** turns plain musical notes and rhythms into smooth waveforms with zero audio dropouts (because **Tally** never pauses for garbage collection!),
- **`modules/resonance/mikrophone/`** (*the firmware that forgets*) records your voice into a bounded ring buffer and cryptographically wipes unkept audio so your privacy stays whole.
