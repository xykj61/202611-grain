Grain is a custody-first civic project built so ordinary people -- families,
small collectives, and local stewards -- keep their own words and tools in
their own hands. How we treat each other matches the software: plain, honest,
and kind.

## The Standard We Hold

- **Lead with kindness and assume good faith:** Read the most generous version
  of what someone wrote before answering. A question is a welcome invitation; a
  mistake is a step toward clarity.
- **Speak honestly and share your reasons:** State what holds true, name gaps
  calmly, and offer clear evidence (`context/TWO_ROOMS.md`). Honesty spoken
  warmly is the whole standard.
- **Prefer *yet* to sharp dismissal:** Engage ideas directly while honoring
  every person with steady dignity.
- **Welcome the newcomer:** Every person arriving on day one belongs here.
  Explain each term clearly (`LEXICON.md`), link live references, and leave
  every door plainer than you found it.
- **Honor custody and consent:** Treat every person's private details, keys,
  and identity as strictly theirs to share by explicit choice.

## Scope & Private Reporting

This standard applies across all Grain repositories, issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and community gatherings. When conduct falls short of this
standard, please report privately through GitHub's private security advisory
channel or direct steward contact listed in [`context/THREATS.md`](context/THREATS.md).
Every report receives attentive human care, proportionate response, and
confidentiality.
